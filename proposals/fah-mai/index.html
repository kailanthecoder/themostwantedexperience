01Overview

One group.
Distinct audiences.

Fah Mai Holdings operates across the spirits industry, connecting corporate services, whisky auctions and cask ownership. The opportunity is to make this wider ecosystem simple to understand and easy to navigate.

Objective

Present the group clearly, while helping each audience reach the relevant business or service.

Approach

A restrained visual system, clear information architecture and purposeful customer journeys.

02Current

Three platforms.
Three clear roles.

Each website serves a different part of the business. A group-wide digital experience should respect those distinctions rather than force every visitor through the same journey.

01
Fah Mai Holdings ↗
Corporate group, bulk spirit trade, distribution and portfolio services.
02
Whisky Bull ↗
Whisky auctions, registration, buying and selling.
03
Platinum Cask ↗
Whisky cask ownership, fractions and bottlings.
Public-facing review only. Technical access, analytics and existing workflows have not yet been audited.
03Spec

Clear requirements.
Purposeful execution.

01

Responsive, legible page layouts and practical mobile navigation.
04
Performance & SEO
Clean technical foundations, search-friendly content and appropriate measurement.
04Proposed

Connected identity.
Independent journeys.

Rather than treating the portfolio as one website, the proposed direction creates a clearer relationship between the group and its specialist destinations.

01
Group front door
Position Fah Mai Holdings as the corporate introduction to the group, its services and capabilities.
02
Auction journey
Preserve Whisky Bull’s focused buying, selling and membership experience.
03
Cask journey
Keep Platinum Cask product discovery and cask ownership content distinct and accessible.
Proposed concept only. Any rebuild of transactional auction, payment or investment systems must be separately scoped.
